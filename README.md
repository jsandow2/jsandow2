Applied microeconomist finishing a PhD at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln in December 2026. I build instrumental variables designs on large public microdata to answer policy questions where the causal claim is contested.

## Research

**[Mother of Exiles, Maker of Scholars](https://github.com/jsandow2/Immigration_Card)** — job market paper

U.S.-born Americans adjust how much schooling they acquire in response to the immigrant inflows of their own formative years. The contribution is in how exposure is built: national arrivals by origin, allocated across metropolitan areas by 1990 settlement shares, resolved across 230 origin groups, and averaged over each person's ages 15–25 — so treatment varies at the metropolitan-area by birth-cohort level across 8.5 million Census and ACS observations, 1990–2023.

**[Do Guns Make Us Safe?](https://github.com/jsandow2/firearm_mortality_IV)** — working paper

Household firearm ownership and firearm mortality in a county-level panel, 1999–2016. Ownership is instrumented with a population-weighted leave-one-out mean of neighboring counties' murder arrest rates, with two-way fixed effects and Anderson–Rubin weak-instrument-robust inference, because the first stage is not strong enough to take conventional inference on faith.

A third chapter, on whether the age at which exposure occurs changes the schooling response, is in progress.
